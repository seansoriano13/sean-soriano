# Sean Soriano — Portfolio

A one-page portfolio for Sean Soriano, Software Engineer (Philippines, open to freelance), opened by a scroll-driven camera dive through a guitar's sound hole that resolves into his portrait.

## Language

### The opening

**Dive**:
The opening scroll sequence: the camera travels from inside the guitar body, through the sound hole and past the strings, until the frame is white.
_Avoid_: intro video, hero video, zoom

**Hero Text**:
All copy laid over the Dive's first frame. It travels with the camera, held over the dark wood, and is gone before the strings fill the frame.
_Avoid_: hero copy, overlay

**Headline**:
One of the two large statement lines in the Hero Text: "Built for real work" (top left) and "Tuned to the detail" (bottom right).
_Avoid_: title, tagline

**Mark**:
Sean's own glasses glyph: a steady round lens ("order") and a slightly tilted, compressed one ("anxiety"), joined by an uneven bridge, with a faded temple line. It is also the favicon.
_Avoid_: icon, glasses icon

**Logo**:
The Mark beside "Sean Soriano", set in dark ink inside the bright sound hole, which settles into the nav as the Dive begins.
_Avoid_: wordmark, brand

**Role Line**:
"Software Engineer", the small title that sits above the About.

**About**:
The short paragraph in the Hero Text saying what Sean builds.
_Avoid_: bio, intro

### The arrival

**Reveal**:
The moment the Dive ends, when Sean's cut-out portrait comes into focus on Premium White, as if the camera's focus is still pulling in.
_Avoid_: outro, end screen

**Premium White**:
The warm ivory (`#F6F4EF`) the Dive resolves into and the page lives on afterwards. Paired with near-black ink and no accent colour.
_Avoid_: white, background

**Portrait Notes**:
The short texts set around the portrait in the Reveal: proof, stack, how Sean works, where he is and whether he's available. Nothing in them repeats the Hero Text.
_Avoid_: about section, bio

### The work

**Work**:
Something Sean designed and built solo, with AI assistance, for real use. There are two, both for T3CK Group: the corporate website (designed from scratch) and the ERP.
_Avoid_: project (unqualified), portfolio piece

**Study**:
A recreation of someone else's design made for practice, always labelled as one. The Anduril recreation is the only Study.
_Avoid_: clone (in visitor-facing copy), project
